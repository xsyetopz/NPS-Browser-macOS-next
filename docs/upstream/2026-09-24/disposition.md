## Status rules

- **Fixed** requires a named check and observed result that directly exercises the reported behavior.
- **Incorporated with modification** records a demonstrated adaptation while naming what remains outside that evidence.
- **Not applicable** requires evidence that the reported path or behavior does not apply to the replacement.
- Keep all other entries **Unverified**. Do not infer resolution of a crash, data-loss, corruption, real-game extraction, or GUI report from a successful build or an adjacent fixture check.
